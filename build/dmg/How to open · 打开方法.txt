FIRE TOAD — HOW TO OPEN

1. Drag FIRE Toad onto the Applications folder beside it.
2. Double-click it in Applications. macOS asks once whether to open an app
   downloaded from the internet: click Open.
3. Choose "Make one" and pick an empty folder. Your browser opens on the setup.

Full guide: https://github.com/lydiablllue/firetoad/blob/main/docs/install.md


FIRE TOAD — 打开方法

1. 把 FIRE Toad 拖到旁边的 Applications（应用程序）文件夹上。
2. 在「应用程序」里双击它。macOS 会问一次是否打开从互联网下载的应用：点「打开」。
3. 选「Make one」，指定一个空文件夹。浏览器会打开建账向导。

完整说明：https://github.com/lydiablllue/firetoad/blob/main/docs/zh/install.md
