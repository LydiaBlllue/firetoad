FIRE Toad
中文

Your money on one page — for reaching financial independence in Canada.

Once a month you drop your bank statements into a folder. FIRE Toad reads them on your Mac, checks that every statement adds up, files every purchase and builds one page: what you spent, what you own, how many years until financial independence.

Open the live demo Download for Mac — free

The demo is a made-up owner's page, nothing to install. The app is signed and notarised by Apple, in English and Chinese, free and open source (MIT).

The demo is built for a computer screen; on a phone, the screenshots below show it.

  • No bank login.You download your statements; the app reads them on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to make.
  • Every number adds up.A statement is let in only when it balances to the totals it prints.
  • Your FIRE plan, not just a budget.Years to financial independence, how long the money lasts, your TFSA, RRSP and FHSA, and money held abroad, in one net worth.

What it reads

RBC and BMO PDF statements, any bank's OFX or QFX download (TD, Scotiabank, CIBC, Tangerine and others offer one), and Wealthsimple's holdings and activities exports. A balance no file carries is typed once a month. Every bank and broker →
