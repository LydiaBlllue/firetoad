JADE TOAD — HOW TO OPEN (the app is not signed yet; this is only the first time)

1. Drag Jade Toad onto the Applications folder beside it.
2. Double-click it in Applications. macOS says it could not verify the app: click Done.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open Anyway,
   confirm with your password. Click Open in the last dialog.
   macOS 14 and earlier: right-click the app → Open is enough.
4. Choose "Make one" and pick an empty folder. Your browser opens on the setup.

Full guide: https://github.com/lydiablllue/jade-toad/blob/main/docs/install.md


JADE TOAD — 打开方法（安装包还没签名；只有第一次这样）

1. 把 Jade Toad 拖到旁边的 Applications（应用程序）文件夹上。
2. 在「应用程序」里双击它。macOS 说无法验证：点「完成」。
3. 打开「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」，拉到最底下，点「仍要打开」，输密码确认。
   最后一个对话框点「打开」。macOS 14 及更早：右键应用 → 打开 即可。
4. 选「Make one」，指定一个空文件夹。浏览器会打开建账向导。

完整说明：https://github.com/lydiablllue/jade-toad/blob/main/docs/zh/install.md
