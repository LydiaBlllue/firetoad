FIRE TOAD — HOW TO OPEN

1. Double-click FIRE Toad in this window. macOS asks once whether to open an app
   downloaded from the internet: click Open.
2. FIRE Toad copies itself into Applications, opens, and closes this window.
3. Click "Create my folder". It is made in Documents, as "FIRE Toad".
   Your browser opens on the setup.

Next time, open FIRE Toad from Applications or Launchpad.

Full guide: https://github.com/lydiablllue/firetoad/blob/main/docs/install.md


FIRE TOAD — 打开方法

1. 在这个窗口里双击 FIRE Toad。macOS 会问一次是否打开从互联网下载的应用：点「打开」。
2. FIRE Toad 会把自己拷进「应用程序」，自动打开，并关掉这个窗口。
3. 点「新建文件夹」。它会建在「文稿」里，名字是「FIRE Toad」。浏览器会打开建账向导。

以后从「应用程序」或启动台打开 FIRE Toad。

完整说明：https://github.com/lydiablllue/firetoad/blob/main/docs/zh/install.md
